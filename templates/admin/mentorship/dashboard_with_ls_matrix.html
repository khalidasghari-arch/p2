Mentorship by Province and Thematic Area

Selected dashboard filters apply to all three indicators. LS entries count repeated sessions. Mentee rows count distinct mentee IDs. PC or MC means at least one recorded competency; it does not mean all topics are completed or that prior LS was verified. Province comes from the visit facility. ANC includes ANC, PMH and MCH Handbook.

Distinct totals are deduplicated, not added: a mentee in two thematic areas counts in each area but only once in the province total. The overall total also deduplicates mentees across provinces. Missing mentee IDs are excluded from mentee counts but their LS entries remain included.
