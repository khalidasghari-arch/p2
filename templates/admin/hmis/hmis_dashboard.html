Delivery Composition

Share of normal, assisted, and C-section deliveries among total reported deliveries.

Calculation notes: Total Deliveries = Normal + Assisted + C-section deliveries. C-section Rate = C-sections ÷ Total Deliveries × 100. Stillbirths per 1,000 = Stillbirths ÷ Total Deliveries × 1,000.
