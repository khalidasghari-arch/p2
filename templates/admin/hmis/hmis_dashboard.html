Calculation and data checks

{{ composite.note }}

LBW: LBW ÷ Newborn Alive × 100. Breastfed 1st Hour: Babies-breastfed-1st-hour ÷ Newborn Alive × 100. NB Resuscitated: Newborn-resuscitated ÷ Newborn Alive × 100. Premature: Premature ÷ Newborn Alive × 100.

These are four separate derived rates. They are not added into a single score because the categories can overlap. Rates above 100% remain visible and are flagged for review.
