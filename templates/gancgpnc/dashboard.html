Postnatal Care Clinical Performance

Newborn breastfeeding and vaccination use the latest attended Group/Individual record per enrollment at each PNC stage. Newborns with a death reported by that assessment are excluded; PNC1 deaths are carried forward to PNC2. Death timing uses the reporting session date. A death in a multiple birth requires review because the Yes/No field does not identify how many babies remain alive. Other clinical indicators continue to use their existing maternal denominators.
